THE ARCHITECTURE EVOLUTION
From intelligence layer
to ARYA Certified™

Our conversation evolved beyond tax optimization into something structurally deeper. The key addition: ARYA doesn't just tell an investor what to do. It certifies the intelligence, authorizes automated execution, and independently audits what the broker actually did.

"Traditional TCA asks: did I get good execution? ARYA asks: did the broker execute the exact independently certified instruction I authorized, when it was supposed to, under the agreed execution rules? That is a different layer entirely."

The Four-Layer Architecture:

Layer 1 — INTELLIGENCE Arya analyzes: portfolio + market + tax + regulatory + execution conditions Layer 2 — CERTIFICATION Arya generates: ARYA Certified™ Signal Signal ID · Investor ID · Instrument · Quantity Execution window · Timestamp · ARYA signature/hash Layer 3 — EXECUTION Investor-authorized broker executes the instruction ARYA never becomes the broker Keys stay with the user — on their Google account Layer 4 — AUDIT ARYA independently reconciles: What ARYA instructed ↔ What the broker executed Produces: ARYA Execution Certificate
